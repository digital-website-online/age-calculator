Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026 | AgePulse Official Portal

At AgePulse, accessible from our official website, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by AgePulse and how we utilize it.

1. Data Processing & Privacy Guarantee

AgePulse operates as a client-side utility application. All calculations (including birth dates, age differences, pet age conversions, and due date estimations) are processed locally within your web browser. We do not store, collect, transmitted, or sell any personal dates or personal identifiers entered into our calculators.

2. Log Files & Traffic Analytics

Like most standard web servers, AgePulse utilizes log files and browser analytics services (such as Google Analytics). The information inside the log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring/exit pages and click counts

This information is purely used for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking user movement on the website, and gathering demographic information to optimize overall user experience.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

AgePulse uses 'Cookies' to store preferences and recording page visits. These cookies optimize the user experience by customizing web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Ads

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

AgePulse's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information regarding their practices and instructions on how to opt-out of certain options.

6. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under CCPA and GDPR regulations, users have rights including:

  • The right to request disclosure of personal data collected.
  • The right to request deletion of any personal data stored.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of personal data.

Since AgePulse does not collect personal identity records or store user-submitted inputs, your identity remains completely anonymous.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
