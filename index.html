How does the age calculator work?

The age calculator subtracts your date of birth from today's date using exact Gregorian calendar arithmetic. It outputs the result in multiple time units simultaneously: years, months, days, weeks, hours, and seconds.

This baseline measurement serves vital roles in medical dosing, legal eligibility, retirement filings, school admissions, and demographic research worldwide. Our system applies ISO 8601:2019 interval arithmetic—the exact same standard used by clinical software and government agencies globally.
